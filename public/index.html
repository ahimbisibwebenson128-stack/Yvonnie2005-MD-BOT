FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Where do I enter the pairing code?On the WhatsApp account being linked, open Settings → Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number instead. Enter the code shown on this page.
How do I use the QR code?Tap Display QR code, then open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device and scan the QR displayed here. Scan it immediately because WhatsApp refreshes QR codes after a short time.
Can I use the pairing code and QR code?Yes. This portal provides both methods. Complete only one linking method for the session; do not request repeated pairing codes while a request is active.
The service says it is starting. What should I do?Wait for the Render service to finish starting, refresh this page, and try again when the bot reports that pairing is ready.
My pairing code expired or WhatsApp rejected it.Do not keep requesting codes. Wait for the previous attempt to end, refresh the portal and check the Render runtime logs before retrying.
Why is the QR unavailable?The bot must first receive a fresh QR from WhatsApp. Wait for the QR-ready state and tap Refresh QR. The QR endpoint requires the owner access key.
Is my owner key stored in the project?No. The portal sends it over HTTPS to verify your request. Never paste it into GitHub or share it with anyone.
